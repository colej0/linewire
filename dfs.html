Why line movement matters for PrizePicks

PrizePicks and other pick'em apps set a projection and pay fixed multipliers, so every pick has to clear roughly a 54–58% hit rate to break even. Sportsbook props move on news and sharp money faster than pick'em projections. When a book's line moves past the PrizePicks number, the no-vig probability on that side can top the breakeven — that's the edge the free Sunday email looks for.
