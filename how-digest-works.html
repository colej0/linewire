How the digest works

From opening lines to your inbox: what we capture, what we filter out, and when each email arrives.

  • Openers captured
  • Moves tracked
  • DFS 3/2/1
  • Free weekly
  • Paid 3x/week

1. Capture the openers

Each morning we record opening spreads, totals and moneylines from public odds data so every later move has a baseline.

2. Track and rank the moves

We compare current numbers to the open, rank the biggest moves, and flag possible steam or reverse line movement. Moves explained by injuries or weather are labeled.

3. Build the DFS section

Picks are formatted for the platforms you choose at signup: PrizePicks, Underdog, DraftKings Pick 6, plus straight bets. The format is 3 core, 2 value, 1 fade.

4. One call, minus the noise

Each issue ends with the single spot most worth your attention and a short list of moves to ignore.

When emails arrive

  • Free: 1 email per week, Sunday morning (top moves, 1-2 DFS notes, one call).
  • Signal ($9) / Edge ($19): 3 emails per week, one full digest plus two shorter updates with DFS picks and line changes.
  • 7-day free trial: all 3 weekly emails.
  • Edge: plus alerts when watchlist lines hit your number.

What we never do

No sportsbook or bank logins. Public data only. Information, not betting advice. 21+.
