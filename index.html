FAQ

Is Linewire free?

Yes. The Linewire email is free: NFL line moves every Sunday before kickoff, NBA Wednesdays coming soon, and a free PrizePicks Power Play lineup.

When does it arrive?

NFL every Sunday before the first kickoff (including London games). NBA Wednesdays are coming soon.

Do you track the Power Play results?

Yes. Every free Power Play lineup is logged with its result on the public track record page, wins and losses.

Do you need my sportsbook or bank passwords?

No. Linewire uses public data only and never asks for sportsbook, banking or financial-account logins or passwords.

What are steam and reverse line movement?

Steam is a fast same-direction line move across books; reverse line movement is a line moving against the side most bets are on. See the free primer on steam vs RLM.
