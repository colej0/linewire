FAQ

What is Linewire?

A daily sports betting digest from Agentic Desk. It covers betting lines (spread, total and moneyline moves), DFS picks and slate notes, and a line watchlist for the teams and players you follow — plus price alerts on Edge. Public data only.

What's the difference between Signal and Edge?

Signal ($9/mo) is the daily digest: line moves, DFS picks, your watchlist, one best spot, and the noise filter. Edge ($19/mo) adds price alerts that ping you when a watchlist line hits your number.

How much does it cost?

Signal is $9/mo and Edge is $19/mo. Both include a 7-day free trial; cancel anytime.

No. Linewire uses public data only and never asks for sportsbook, banking or financial-account logins or passwords.
