What is a steam move?

A steam move is a fast, same-direction line change across multiple sportsbooks in a short window, usually driven by large or respected wagers. Example: a spread going from -2.5 to -3.5 at several books within minutes.

What is reverse line movement (RLM)?

RLM is when a line moves against the side receiving the majority of public bets. If most tickets are on the favorite but the line drops from -7 to -6, books may be reacting to money on the underdog.

How to read them

  • Compare to the opener. A move only means something relative to where the line started.
  • Watch key numbers. In the NFL, moves through 3 and 7 matter more than 4.5 to 5.
  • Check the news first. Injuries and weather explain many moves that look like steam.
  • Don't chase stale numbers. By the time a move is visible, the best price is often gone. Set a target and wait.
