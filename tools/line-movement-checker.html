FAQ

What does line movement mean in NFL betting?

Line movement is the change from the opening spread or total to the current one. A spread moving toward a team means the market now rates that team higher (bettors who took the other side early got a better number). A total dropping means the market expects less scoring.

Why do NFL betting lines move?

Mostly news and money: injuries (especially quarterbacks), weather like strong wind, and bets from sharp bettors that books respect. Moves through key numbers like 3 and 7 matter most, because so many NFL games end on those margins.

Where does this line data come from?

DraftKings opening and current lines from ESPN’s public NFL odds feed, refreshed by Linewire during the week. Games that are already final are removed. We show what moved, not why, so check injury and weather news before you bet.
