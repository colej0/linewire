FAQ

What does line movement mean in NFL betting?

Line movement is the change from the opening spread, total or moneyline to the current one. A spread moving toward a team means the market now rates that team higher, so bettors who took the other side early got a better number. A total dropping means the market expects less scoring.

Why do NFL betting lines move?

Mostly news and money: injuries (especially quarterbacks), weather like strong wind, and bets from sharp bettors that books respect. Moves through key numbers like 3 and 7 matter most, because so many NFL games end on those margins.

Which opening line does this checker use?

The game-week opener: the DraftKings line in effect at 00:00 CT on the Tuesday the game week starts, taken from DraftKings’ timestamped line history (via Action Network’s public odds data). Many sites show the spring “lookahead” line as the open, which can exaggerate moves; we don’t. Current lines are the latest DraftKings numbers.
