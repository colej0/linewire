FAQ

How is expected value calculated?

EV = (win probability × profit if win) − (loss probability × stake). At +150 with a 42% win chance, EV on $10 is 0.42×15 − 0.58×10 = +$0.50.

What is the Kelly criterion?

Kelly sizes a bet as edge ÷ net odds: f = (p×b − (1−p)) ÷ b, where b is decimal odds minus 1. Many bettors use half or quarter Kelly because win probabilities are estimates.

Where does my win probability come from?

It's your estimate. A common reference is the no-vig probability from a sharp market — use the odds converter or hold calculator to get one.
