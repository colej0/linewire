FAQ

Is Power or Flex better on PrizePicks?

It depends on your hit rate per pick. At standard payouts Power has higher EV above these crossover hit rates: 2-pick 50.0%, 3-pick 50.0%, 4-pick 60.0%, 5-pick 56.6%, 6-pick 56.9%. Below the crossover, Flex loses less (or wins more) because it still pays with one or two misses.

What hit rate do I need for Flex to be profitable?

Per pick: 57.7% on a 3-pick Flex, 55.0% on 4, 54.3% on 5 and 54.2% on 6. Power needs 55.0%, 56.2%, 54.9% and 54.7% for the same sizes.

Why does Flex feel better even when Power has higher EV?

Flex pays more often, so the bankroll swings are smaller. Power pays only when every pick hits, so it is streakier. If you are close to breakeven or unsure of your hit rate, Flex is the lower-variance choice; if you are confident you are well above 55%, Power usually pays more.
