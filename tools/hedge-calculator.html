When does hedging make sense?

Hedging trades upside for certainty. It is most useful on long-shot futures and the last leg of a parlay, where one result decides a big payout. Each hedge pays the book’s vig again, so on average it costs you a little EV — hedge when the guaranteed amount matters more to you than the extra upside. FAQ

How do you calculate a hedge bet?

To lock the same profit whichever side wins, hedge stake = original stake × original decimal odds ÷ hedge decimal odds. A $20 bet at +800 (9.00) hedged at -150 (1.667) needs $108, which locks +$52 either way.

How much should I hedge to break even?

To get your money back if the original bet loses, hedge stake = original stake ÷ (hedge decimal odds − 1). For a $20 bet hedged at -150 that is $30: you lose nothing if the hedge wins and keep +$130 if the original wins.

Is hedging better than cashing out?

Compare the cash-out offer with the profit a hedge locks. Books build margin into cash-out offers, so a hedge at a fair price usually locks more. Enter the offer above and the calculator shows which is worth more.
