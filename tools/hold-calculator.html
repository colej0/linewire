How to use this calculator

Type the American odds for both sides of a market (a spread, total, moneyline or prop). For a soccer 3-way or any market with a draw, fill in side 3. The green or red line grades the hold, and the table shows each side’s fair probability and fair odds. Copy the result to share a market.

FAQ

What is hold in sports betting?

Hold is the share of the money a sportsbook expects to keep on a market if bets are balanced: 1 − 1 ÷ (sum of implied probabilities). A -110/-110 market has about 4.55% hold.

How do you calculate the vig on a bet?

Convert each side to implied probability (e.g. -110 = 52.38%), add them up (104.76%), and the hold is 1 − 1 ÷ 1.0476 = 4.55%. To remove the vig, divide each side’s implied probability by the total.

What is a good hold percentage?

Lower is better for you. Under 3% is sharp (reduced-juice or very liquid markets), 4–5% is standard for spreads and totals, and player props, futures and parlays often run 8–30%. Compare books before you bet.
