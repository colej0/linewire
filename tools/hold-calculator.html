FAQ

What is hold in sports betting?

Hold is the share of total money a sportsbook expects to keep on a market if bets are balanced: 1 − 1/(sum of implied probabilities). A -110/-110 market has about 4.55% hold.

How do you remove the vig?

Divide each side's implied probability by the sum of all sides' implied probabilities. The results add to 100% and are the no-vig (fair) probabilities.

What is a good hold?

Lower is better for the bettor. Standard -110/-110 sides are about 4.5%; props and futures often carry much higher hold. Compare books and markets before betting.
