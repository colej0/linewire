FAQ

How do you convert American odds to implied probability?

For negative odds: |odds| ÷ (|odds| + 100). For positive odds: 100 ÷ (odds + 100). Example: -150 is 60%, +150 is 40%, and -110 is 52.38%.

How do you convert American odds to decimal odds?

Positive odds: 1 + odds ÷ 100. Negative odds: 1 + 100 ÷ |odds|. Example: +200 is 3.00, -200 is 1.50, -110 is 1.91. Fractional odds are decimal minus 1, so 3.00 is 2/1. Both +100 and -100 are even money: 2.00 decimal, 1/1.

What is the vig and how do you remove it?

The vig (juice) is the bookmaker’s margin. In a two-way market the implied probabilities add up to more than 100%. Divide each side’s implied probability by the total to get the no-vig (fair) probability; -110/-110 becomes 50%/50%, or even money.
