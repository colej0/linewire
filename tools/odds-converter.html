FAQ

How do you convert American odds to implied probability?

For negative odds: |odds| / (|odds| + 100). For positive odds: 100 / (odds + 100). Example: -150 is 60%, +150 is 40%.

How do you convert American odds to decimal odds?

Positive odds: 1 + odds/100. Negative odds: 1 + 100/|odds|. Example: +200 = 3.00, -200 = 1.50.

What is the vig and how do you remove it?

The vig (juice) is the bookmaker's margin. In a two-way market the implied probabilities add up to more than 100%. Divide each side's implied probability by the total to get the no-vig (fair) probability.

How do prediction-market prices map to odds?

A $0.40 Yes contract implies a 40% probability, which is about +150 in American odds or 2.50 in decimal odds, before fees.
