How to use this calculator

Pick your slip size, enter the hit rate you honestly expect per pick (55% is a strong long-run number), and your entry. The green line tells you which play pays more and whether it is +EV. Why line movement matters for PrizePicks

PrizePicks projections often lag sportsbook moves. When a book prop moves past the PrizePicks line, the no-vig probability on that side can clear the ~55% breakeven. FAQ

What hit rate do you need to break even on a PrizePicks Power Play?

At standard payouts, each pick needs to hit 57.7% on a 2-pick Power (3x), 55.0% on 3 picks (6x), 56.2% on 4 picks (10x), 54.9% on 5 picks (20x) and 54.7% on 6 picks (37.5x), assuming independent picks.

What hit rate do you need to break even on a Flex Play?

Flex breakevens per pick are 61.8% (2-pick), 57.7% (3), 55.0% (4), 54.3% (5) and 54.2% (6). Flex pays with one or two misses, so it lowers variance, but it needs a higher hit rate than Power on 2–3 pick slips.

How does a PrizePicks breakeven compare to sportsbook odds?

A 54.9% breakeven per pick is like laying -122 on every leg. A standard -110/-110 prop is 50% no-vig, so you need props where the sharp side is priced around -122 or shorter to beat a 5-pick Power. Open the no-vig section above to check a specific prop.
