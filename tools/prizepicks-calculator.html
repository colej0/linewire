Why line movement matters for PrizePicks

PrizePicks projections often lag sportsbook moves. When a book prop moves past the PrizePicks line, the no-vig probability on that side can clear the ~55% breakeven. The free Sunday email flags those moves; see steam vs reverse line movement and this week's NFL line moves.

FAQ

What is the breakeven win rate per pick on a PrizePicks Power Play?

At standard payouts, a 2-pick Power (3x) needs 57.7% per pick, 3-pick (6x) 55.0%, 4-pick (10x) 56.2%, 5-pick (20x) 54.9% and 6-pick (37.5x) 54.7%, assuming independent picks.

Is Power or Flex better on PrizePicks?

It depends on your per-pick hit rate. Flex lowers variance by paying with one or two misses, but at standard multipliers Flex breakevens run from 61.8% (2-pick) down to 54.2% (6-pick) per pick — worse than Power on 2–3 picks, slightly better on 4–6. Use the calculator to compare EV at your own hit rate.

How does breakeven compare to sportsbook odds?

A standard -110/-110 prop has a no-vig fair price of 50%, while -110 itself implies 52.4%. PrizePicks breakevens of roughly 54–58% per pick are equivalent to laying about -119 to -136 per leg.

Are these PrizePicks payouts current?

They are PrizePicks' publicly documented standard Player Pick rates as checked Oct 16, 2026 (Power 2–6 picks: 3x, 6x, 10x, 20x, 37.5x). Demons, Goblins, correlated picks and promos change payouts, and PrizePicks may change rates at any time. Always check the payout shown in the app.
