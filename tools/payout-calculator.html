FAQ

How is a moneyline payout calculated?

Payout = stake × decimal odds, and profit = payout − stake. On a +150 underdog, $10 pays $25 ($15 profit). On a -150 favorite, $10 pays $16.67 ($6.67 profit).

What does a -110 spread bet pay?

At -110 you risk $110 to win $100. A $10 spread bet at -110 returns $19.09 total, a $9.09 profit. You need to win 52.38% of -110 bets to break even.

How much do I need to bet to win $100?

On negative odds, bet the number: -110 needs $110, -200 needs $200. On positive odds, bet 100 ÷ odds × 100: +150 needs $66.67, +200 needs $50. Fill in the “Want to win” box for any price.
