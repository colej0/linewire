FAQ

How is a bet payout calculated?

Payout = stake × decimal odds. Profit = payout − stake. Example: $50 at +150 (2.50) pays $125, a $75 profit.

How do you calculate a parlay payout?

Multiply the decimal odds of every leg, then multiply by the stake. Two legs at -110 (1.909 each) make about 3.64, so $10 pays about $36.45.

What does a -110 line pay?

At -110 you risk $110 to win $100. A $10 bet returns $19.09 total ($9.09 profit).

Does this include taxes or fees?

No. It shows the gross return at the odds you enter. Sportsbook, DFS and prediction-market fees and taxes vary.
