How a round robin works

A round robin turns one list of picks into several smaller parlays. "By 2's" on 4 picks makes every possible 2-team parlay: 6 tickets. "By 3's" makes 4 three-team parlays. You pay the stake on every ticket, so the total risk climbs fast, but one losing pick no longer kills the whole bet the way it does in a single parlay. The trade-off is that each ticket still carries the book's vig, and a round robin is just many parlays at once. It changes how your results swing, not your expected value per dollar.

FAQ

How many bets are in a round robin?

A round robin by k's on n picks has C(n, k) parlays, the number of ways to choose k picks from n. 4 picks by 2's = 6 parlays, 5 by 2's = 10, 5 by 3's = 10, 6 by 3's = 20. Your total stake is that count times the stake per parlay.

How is a round robin paid?

Each parlay inside the round robin is graded on its own. A parlay pays stake × the product of its legs' decimal odds when all of its legs win, and loses the stake otherwise. Your result is the sum of every cashed parlay minus your total stake.

Is a round robin better than a parlay?

Not in expected value: it is several parlays, and each one carries the vig. A round robin lowers your risk of losing everything on one bad leg, but the most it can pay is smaller than one big parlay with the same total stake. Use it when you want a smoother result, not a bigger edge.
